Donate

Every dollar keeps the Kitchen open to everyone.

We’re run entirely by volunteers, so your donation goes straight into the space, the tools, and the community.

Give online

Donate with PayPal @bicyclekitchen · Use a card or your PayPal account. Donate with Venmo @BicycleKitchen · Quick and easy from your phone. Donate with Zeffy Free for nonprofits, so more of your gift reaches us.

Bicycle Kitchen is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

Need a tax receipt? Email us at kitchen@bicyclekitchen.com with proof of your donation, and we’ll send one your way.

Where your money goes

Here’s what your donation actually pays for.

Keeping the space open

The bills that keep our doors open, so there’s a place to come wrench every week.

Tools

Buying, replacing, and repairing the tools and stands everyone uses, from tire levers to truing stands.

Community events

Showing up for events like CicLAvia to help riders and bring bike repair out into the streets.

Staying accessible

No one is turned away for lack of funds. Your donation covers those who can’t pay, so the Kitchen stays open to everyone.

Other ways to give

Donate bikes and parts

Drop off used bikes and parts during open hours. They become project bikes and spare parts for the community. Tax receipts available.

Give your time
