Bicycle Kitchen is a volunteer-run DIY bike workspace on Fountain Ave. Use our tools, find used parts, and learn to fix your own bike, whatever your skill level.

What we do

Photo: someone truing a wheel at a stand

Open shop

Get help with your own bike or build one from donated frames and parts. We provide tools and guidance. New and used parts available for purchase.

How open shop works See when we’re open
Photo: donated bikes lined up and waiting for new riders

Project bikes

Pick one of our donated bikes, agree on a price, and build it up yourself with any used part in the shop. We work with any budget.

How project bikes work

Volunteer

No experience needed. Shadow a Cook, sort parts, repair donations, design flyers, or help however you can!

How to get started

Our mission

To promote the bicycle as a fun, safe, and accessible form of transportation, to foster healthy urban communities, and to provide a welcoming space to learn about building, maintaining, and riding bicycles.

Keep the Kitchen running

We’re run 100% by volunteers. Donations keep our doors open, our tool wall stocked, and bikes going to neighbors who need them.
