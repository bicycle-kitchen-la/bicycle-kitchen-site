Volunteer

Bicycle Kitchen runs entirely on volunteers. Every open shop happens because someone showed up.

No experience needed. If you can show up, we can teach you the rest.

How to get started See shop hours

When we’re short on volunteers, we close.

Every extra pair of hands means more open nights for neighbors who need their bike fixed and a place to learn how.

Ways to help

Pick whatever fits you. Plenty of volunteers start with one job and pick up more over time.

Shadow a Cook

Cooks are the volunteers who run open shop. Work alongside one to learn the tools and the stands, and how to help visitors fix their own bikes.

Sort parts

Keep the parts bins organized so visitors can find what they need. It’s a great first job and a crash course in how bikes go together.

Repair donations

Fix up donated bikes so they’re ready to become project bikes or go to someone who needs a ride.

Design flyers

Help spread the word with flyers, social posts, and signs around the shop.

Help however you can

Translation, outreach, events, fundraising, or something we haven’t thought of yet. If you have a skill to share, we’d love to hear about it.

How to get started

1

Stop by during open hours

Come by any open shop night or Saturday. Check the schedule first, since hours can change.

See this week’s hours

2

Tell a Cook you want to help

Introduce yourself to whoever’s on shift. They’ll show you around and find you something to do.

3

Keep coming back

The more shifts you join, the more you’ll learn, and the more nights we can keep the doors open.

Thursday is FTWNB night. Femme, trans, women and non-binary folks are especially welcome to come learn how to volunteer on Thursday evenings.

Questions before you come by?

Give us a call or send an email, and a volunteer will get back to you.
