Other LA Co-Ops

We’re not the only DIY bike shop in town. Bikerowave and Bike Oven are volunteer-run co-ops that work a lot like we do: you fix your bike, and volunteers show you how.

If one of them is closer to you, or open when we’re closed, go see them. We’re all on the same team.

When we’re closed, they might be open

Friday or Sunday? Bike Oven is open 12 – 5 pm.
Weekend mornings? Bikerowave opens at 10 am Saturday and Sunday.
Late weeknight? Bikerowave stays open until 10 pm, Monday – Thursday.
Live on the Westside? Bikerowave in Mar Vista is likely closer.

Like us, they’re volunteer-run, so hours can change. Check with them before you go.

Mar Vista, Westside · Since 2007

Bikerowave

An all-volunteer bike repair co-op serving the Westside since 2007.

Bikerowave gives you the space, tools, and know-how to fix your own bike, from patching a flat to building a wheel. Volunteers restore donated bikes and sell them at fair prices, and no one is turned away for lack of funds. It’s run democratically by its members, a lot like us.

Highland Park, Northeast LA · Since 2005

Bike Oven

A volunteer-run workspace that grew from a one-car garage in Highland Park.

Bike Oven started in 2005 in a single-car garage and moved to its Figueroa St storefront in 2007. Pay for stand time and you get every tool plus advice from volunteer mechanics. They also have restored bikes for sale, lots of used parts, and project bikes they’ll help you build. No one is turned away for lack of funds.
