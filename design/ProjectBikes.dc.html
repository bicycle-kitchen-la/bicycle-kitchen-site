Project bikes

Build your next bike yourself, from one of our many donations.

Anyone can come in, get connected with a donated bike, and work on it as their own project, with tools, used parts, and a Cook to help.

How it works About Work Trade
Photo: donated bikes lined up and waiting for new riders

How it works

1

Pick a bike

Come in during open hours, and a Cook will connect you with one of our donated bikes.

See shop hours

2

Agree on a price

Together, we come to a shared understanding of what your project bike costs.

3

Make it yours

Work on it, and swap in any used part available in our shop. It’s all covered by the price you agreed on.

4

Finish in 30 days

You have 30 days to finish your project. After that, the bike may go back into circulation for someone else.

We work with any budget

Work Trade

Some of your bike’s price can be worked off. Support the Kitchen by helping with tasks like cleaning and organizing the shop, and every hour you put in takes $15 off your project bike.

Just ask a Cook during open hours to set it up.

$15
off per hour of Work Trade

For example, 4 hours of cleaning and organizing takes $60 off your bike’s price.

Good to know

Who can get a project bike?

Anyone. You don’t need experience. Cooks will help you along the way.

What does the price include?

The bike plus any used part available in our shop. Swap or change parts as much as you need for the price you agreed on.

What if I can’t finish in 30 days?

After 30 days, the bike risks going back into circulation so someone else can use it. Talk to a Cook if you’re running short on time.

Can I pay with Work Trade?

Yes. Every hour of Work Trade, like cleaning or organizing, takes $15 off your bike’s price.
